# Helper Functions vs Business Logic

## Helper Function

A reusable function that performs a generic task.

Examples:
- updateElementText()
- formatDate()
- debounce()

---

## Business Logic

A function that solves a problem specific to the application.

Examples:
- getWeatherData()
- showLoadingState()
- saveSearchHistory()

---

## Why separate them?

- Easier to understand
- Easier to maintain
- Easier to reuse
- Better project organization

---

## Revision

Helpers are generic.

Business logic is application-specific.
